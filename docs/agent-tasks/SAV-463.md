## Intent

- User request: investigate why authenticated interviews start much more slowly than the landing-page demo, supervise the fix, and deliver a merge-ready PR.

## Scope

- In scope: keep the per-user ledger lock and atomic session/credit reservation; replace the launch path's per-reservation N+1 audit with a fixed-query exhaustive snapshot before mutation and a fixed-query active-state snapshot afterward; retain fail-closed allocation, reserve-transaction, terminal-history, grant-balance, and rollover evidence; add history- and active-state-scaling regressions; run billing and interview-start validation.
- Out of scope: changing credit policy, trial amounts, Stripe behavior, LiveKit capacity, transcript durability, launch timeouts, customer-facing UI, provider deployment, or Production rollout.
- Assumptions: terminal reservations do not contribute to current `reservedCredits`; their canonical settlement evidence must still be proved before a new reservation can spend the recorded balance.

- Audience: authenticated PM candidates starting a full mock interview.
- Activation/retention/monetization/growth lever: reduce time-to-first-interview without weakening entitlement enforcement.
- Product Compass note: the change keeps Meelni's trustworthy live-practice contract intact and removes internal bookkeeping latency only.

- Likely app files: none.
- Likely package/workflow files: `lib/billing/credits.ts`, `lib/billing/credit-ledger-store.ts`, billing contract/tests, and focused interview-start regressions. Both reuse the existing TypeScript evidence rules.
- Data or provider dependencies: Supabase/Postgres credit-ledger rows; read-only Vercel stage telemetry and LiveKit deployment status informed the diagnosis.
- Docs/decisions to update: this intake and Linear evidence; no durable product decision change expected.
