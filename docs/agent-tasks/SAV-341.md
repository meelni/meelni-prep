# SAV-341 — Dashboard history, analytics, and settings quality

## Intent

- User request: Audit and simplify Interview History, Analytics, and Settings
  from design, interaction, and user perspectives, including important dialog
  states.
- Product/user outcome: Candidates can find reviewable interviews, understand
  evidence-backed progress, and manage their account without redundant
  hierarchy or unstable navigation.
- Why now: Follow-up review found repeated recommendations, collapsed chart
  visuals, moving settings navigation, and overly dense destructive-action copy.

## Slice

- Primary slice: Authenticated dashboard information architecture and design
  quality.
- Secondary slices: Responsive containment, scroll ownership, settings dialog
  accessibility, and browser regression coverage.
- Relevant skills: `meelni-ui-design-system`,
  `meelni-design-review-orchestrator`, `meelni-linear-workstream`, and
  `meelni-pr-prep`.

## Linear Tracking

- Project: Meelni.
- Parent slice issue: SAV-340.
- Child deliverable issue: SAV-341.
- Current Linear state: In Progress.

## Scope

- In scope: Simplify History around the interview log; remove duplicated
  Analytics focus content and repair observed-score bars; stabilize Settings
  navigation and content scrolling; make the deletion dialog concise and
  scannable; add deterministic browser fixtures and coverage.
- Out of scope: New scoring, feedback lifecycle, account deletion, billing,
  authentication, or settings persistence behavior.
- Assumptions: Existing dashboard shell, APIs, data contracts, and semantic
  tokens remain authoritative.

## Product Lens

- Audience: PM candidates returning to review evidence, assess progress, or
  manage practice/account preferences.
- Activation/retention lever: Reduce interpretation cost between entering a
  dashboard page and taking the page's core action.
- Product Compass note: Log first on History; evidence and one next focus on
  Analytics; stable local navigation on Settings.

## Technical Plan

- App files: `components/dashboard/sections/history.tsx`,
  `components/dashboard/sections/analytics.tsx`,
  `components/dashboard/sections/settings.tsx`, and the shared progress-signal
  bars.
- Test files: Browser harness fixtures, synthetic fetch, SAV-341 responsive
  browser spec, and dashboard surface guardrails.
- Data or provider dependencies: Existing insights, sessions, settings,
  billing, export, and deletion endpoints only.
- Docs/decisions to update: This bounded task record and screenshot-backed
  audit. No ADR or OpenSpec is required because provider and product behavior
  do not change.

## Validation

- Required commands: Typecheck, test typecheck, lint, formatting, design
  guardrails, and browser-fixture boundary verification.
- Manual checks: 390px, 768px, and 1280px; no horizontal overflow; visible
  observed-score bars; Settings content-only scrolling; deletion-dialog initial
  focus, dismiss path, and trigger focus restoration.
- Fixtures: Five mixed feedback-history states and three scored analytics
  interviews with deterministic insight scores.

## Rollout

- Risk level: Low.
- Backward compatibility: Existing routes, actions, APIs, and state truth remain
  supported.
- Metrics/events to watch: History-to-feedback review, Analytics-to-practice,
  settings save failure, and deletion-request failure events when available.
- Follow-up slice: None required for the scoped quality cleanup.
