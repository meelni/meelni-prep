# SAV-182 — Durable contact submissions

## Intent

- User request: Continue relaunch-readiness work and resolve open launch issues.
- Product/user outcome: A visitor can submit a support request only when Meelni has durably stored it for support triage.
- Why now: The contact page/API were intentionally unavailable until support intake could avoid false delivery claims.

## Slice

- Primary slice: Public contact/support intake.
- Secondary slices: Privacy/security, public hygiene, launch operations.
- Relevant skill(s): `meelni-linear-workstream`, `meelni-pr-prep`, `meelni-ops-connections`.

## Linear Tracking

- Project: Meelni.
- Parent slice issue: SAV-164.
- Child deliverable issue: SAV-182.
- Current Linear state: In Progress while PR is open.

## Scope

- In scope:
  - Public `/contact` form for account, billing, interview, feedback, privacy, and other support requests.
  - JSON-only same-origin API.
  - Durable Postgres submission queue.
  - Stable idempotency receipts for exact retries.
  - HMAC-scoped IP/email/idempotency hashes for abuse controls.
  - Service-role-only RLS access.
- Out of scope:
  - Email delivery, response-time promises, refunds, or legal-policy approval.
  - Indexing the contact page.
  - Production migration application.
- Assumptions:
  - `DEMO_ABUSE_HMAC_SECRET` remains the existing server-side abuse-control HMAC secret and is domain-separated for contact use.
  - Contact page should stay noindex until the legal/contact identity work is approved.

## Product Lens

- Audience: Prospects and signed-in customers who need account/product/privacy help.
- Activation/retention/monetization/growth lever: Trust and billing/support readiness.
- Product Compass note: Success means “stored for support triage,” not “emailed,” “answered,” or “refund approved.”
