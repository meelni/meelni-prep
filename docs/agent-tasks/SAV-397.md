# SAV-397 Agent Task Intake

## Intent

- User request: Repair the transcript sequence collision and make every
  completed interview reach a truthful evidence-based feedback outcome.
- Product outcome: A trustworthy answer gets useful grounded feedback; silence
  gets a concise no-answer state; transcript failure remains a system state.
- Why now: A sanitized 6m14s Production-shaped trace exposed a turn/marker
  sequence collision and misleading skipped-feedback presentation.

## Slice

- Primary slice: Feedback and evaluation.
- Secondary slices: LiveKit transcript runtime, durable end/dispatch lifecycle,
  API/view presentation, fixtures, and operational taxonomy.
- Relevant skills: Feedback evaluation, LiveKit interview runtime, vertical
  slice playbooks, Linear workstream, engineering memory, codebase
  architecture, and PR preparation.

## Scope

- In scope: Shared writer sequence allocation; evidence-gated eligibility;
  scoreless evidence-limited feedback; typed durable outcomes; provider-free
  production-shaped lifecycle fixtures; safe test-script replacement; API/UI,
  contract, OpenSpec, and regression coverage.
- Out of scope: Production/provider/customer mutation, launch authorization,
  rubric score calibration changes, or relaxing transcript trust controls.
- Assumptions: Frozen transcript evidence remains immutable. A provider-free
  fake evaluator may exercise the real workflow seams without proving provider
  credentials or latency.

## Product Lens

- Audience: PM candidates completing authenticated voice practice.
- Activation/retention/monetization/growth lever: Trust and retention after the
  interview by making feedback accurate, useful, and actionable.
- Product Compass note: Prefer honest evidence-limited coaching over invented
  precision; never make a system failure sound like candidate failure.

## Technical Plan

- Use one runtime allocator for turn and marker sequences with stable replay
  identity.
- Remove duration and total-turn gates; route narrow healthy evidence to a
  scoreless result that retains Feedback v2 grounding.
- Persist the closed outcome taxonomy and map it through worker, API, view
  model, UI, and content-free telemetry.
- Exercise the canonical lifecycle from writer registration through immutable
  persistence and presentation with deterministic fixtures.
- Update feedback, transcript, completion, architecture, decision, and
  OpenSpec memory.
