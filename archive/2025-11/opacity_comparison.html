Key Findings:

  • 5% opacity is barely visible - especially when switching quickly between items
  • 10% opacity provides clear feedback while maintaining subtle aesthetics
  • 15% opacity is very clear but might be too strong for minimalist design
  • Text color change (gray → black/white) helps but isn't enough alone
  • Left border accent (2px gradient) provides additional visual cue

Recommendation:

Change bg-primary/5 to bg-primary/10 for optimal balance between subtlety and visibility.
