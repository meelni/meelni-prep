# Product Lenses

Use these lenses in order. Skip a lens when it is not relevant.

## 1. Problem Definition Lens

Clarify:

- current framing
- struggling moment
- target user
- current workaround
- why this matters now

Flag:

- solution-first thinking
- generic interview-prep thinking
- AI-first thinking without a user problem
- enterprise surface area before buyer signal
- broadening beyond PMs before the PM wedge is working

## 2. PM Interview Wedge Lens

Pressure test whether the idea strengthens Meelni's wedge:

- Does it improve realistic PM mock interviews?
- Does it make the next practice action clearer?
- Does it improve PM-calibrated feedback?
- Does it create better company, question, or round-type specificity?
- Does it improve visible, evidence-based practice progress?

## 3. Growth And Monetization Lens

Name the primary lever:

- acquisition
- activation
- retention
- monetization
- enterprise validation
- product quality

For trials and pricing, tie the decision to activation moments:

- first mock interview completed
- first feedback viewed
- first focused-practice activity started
- first return session
- first paid-intent signal

## 4. AI Product Strategy Lens

Use when AI, voice agents, agent skills, evals, or applied AI PM practice are central.

Pressure test:

- problem first, not AI first
- human-agent boundary
- failure and recovery behavior
- durable product surface, not prompt-dependent trick
- eval or quality signal
- connection back to mock interviews

## 5. Tradeoff Evaluation Lens

Make the decision explicit:

- what are we optimizing for?
- what is the horizon?
- what is the all-in cost?
- what becomes harder later?
- who benefits and who does not?
- what are we explicitly not doing?

Useful prompts:

- Would we start this today with what we know now?
- Is this reversible?
- Is this order-of-magnitude better or just interesting?
- Does this help the PM wedge now or only a future platform story?
