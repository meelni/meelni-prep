# Meelni Product Strategy

Use this skill for product direction and strategy work. Keep recommendations tied to Meelni's actual assets: PM interview focus, question bank, company pages, voice practice, feedback, progress tracking, and existing billing/dashboard surfaces.

## Read First

- `AGENTS.md`
- `.claude/PRD.md`
- `docs/decisions/current.md`
- `docs/architecture/target-monorepo.md`
- Relevant product or SEO docs under `docs/`
- Current product surfaces in `app/`, `components/sections/`, `app/explore/`, and `app/companies/`

## Strategy Defaults

- PM interview prep is the beachhead unless the user explicitly asks to broaden.
- Product Sense is the cleanest acquisition wedge.
- The growth loop is: question/company SEO -> selected question practice -> live rep -> feedback artifact -> return practice.
- Avoid generic AI mock interview positioning unless there is a specific distribution reason.
- Prefer measurable relaunch bets over broad platform expansion.

## Output

Separate:

- what the codebase already supports;
- strategic recommendation;
- assumptions;
- next validation step;
- product or engineering decisions worth recording.
